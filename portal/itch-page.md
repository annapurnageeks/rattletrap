## Description

**Climb the hills. Don't land on your head.**

Drive a rickety little truck as far as you can over endless hills. Hold **Gas** to go and **Brake** to slow down or reverse. In the air, Gas tips you back and Brake tips you forward. Grab fuel cans before the tank runs dry, collect coins, and earn bonus coins for flips, big jumps and wheelies.

**6 adventures**
- Countryside: rolling hills and crates to smash
- Canyon: wobbly rope bridges over the river
- Arctic: snow and slippery ice
- Midnight Woods: pitch dark, only your headlights and the fireflies
- Volcano: bridges over lava
- The Moon: low gravity, huge jumps, endless flips

**4 vehicles**: the Rattletrap truck, a Dirt Bike, a Tractor and a Monster Truck, each with its own handling, 40 garage upgrades and paint jobs.

Plus missions, medals, a daily gift, stats and music. Your progress saves in your browser. No ads, no sign-up.

Controls: on-screen pedals (touch or mouse), or arrow keys / A and D. P pauses.

Made with planck.js physics. Sounds by Kenney (CC0), music "Exploration Theme" by Cleyton Kauffman (CC0), font Nunito (OFL). Open source: https://github.com/annapurnageeks/rattletrap
