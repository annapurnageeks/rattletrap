Rattletrap privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Rattletrap is a driving game made by annapurnageeks. This page explains what information the game uses. In short: the game itself collects nothing about you; the Android app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device information to show them.

What the game stores

Your progress (coins, upgrades, best distances, settings) is saved only on your own device. There are no accounts, no sign-up, and the game does not send your progress anywhere. Deleting the app (or clearing the browser's site data) deletes it.

Ads in the Android app

The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob: an optional ad you can choose to watch to double your coins, and an occasional short ad between runs (never while you drive). To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, general location (country or city), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

  • Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent form.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

The web and portal versions

The version on this website shows no ads and collects nothing. Versions on game websites (such as itch.io) are also subject to that website's own privacy policy.

Children

Rattletrap is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions? Open an issue at github.com/annapurnageeks/rattletrap/issues.
